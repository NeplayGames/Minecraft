# 🟩 Simple Minecraft Clone

This is a **simple Minecraft clone** built with Unity. In this game, you can choose from 3 different blocks, drag and drop them into the scene, and expand your creation. The game offers basic building mechanics, letting you freely place blocks in the environment. To save your progress, just hit the **ESC** button.

## 🧱 Features:
- **Block Selection**: Choose from 3 types of blocks to create your own structures.
- **Drag and Drop**: Easily drag and drop blocks into the scene for simple and intuitive building.
- **Save Feature**: Press **ESC** to save your progress and continue later.
- **Expandable Scenes**: Build and expand the world with your chosen blocks.
